Loung Me Hospital Public Update

### Free Cancer Screening Campaign for Phnom Penh Residents

ថ្ងៃទី ១៨ ខែមេសា ឆ្នាំ ២០២៥

Loung Me Hospital is pleased to announce a free cancer screening campaign for residents of Phnom Penh. This initiative is designed to support early detection, improve public awareness, and make essential health services more accessible to all families in the capital.

The campaign will include screening for common cancer indicators, basic consultation with specialists, and health education on preventive care. Residents are encouraged to register in advance and attend the evaluation sessions during the scheduled dates.

Early detection remains one of the most effective tools in reducing the burden of cancer and improving long-term treatment outcomes. By offering this service free of charge, we hope to make a meaningful contribution to community health and strengthen trust in preventive medical care.

We invite everyone, especially adults and those with a family history of chronic illness, to participate in this important program. Together, we can protect lives through timely screening and better health awareness.

Important Notice

Please register in advance and keep your information ready for a smoother screening experience and direct consultation with our specialists.
